The System

Three-Layer Architecture

Each layer targets a distinct category of AI compute waste.

Layer 01
Semantic Caching
Vectorising inputs to serve cached responses for repeat queries. Zero compute cost.
Layer 02
Intelligent Routing
Directing simple tasks to fast models and reasoning to heavy models. Token efficiency maximised.
Layer 03
Agentic Chunking
Deconstructing complex requests into pipelines to prevent model hallucination.
